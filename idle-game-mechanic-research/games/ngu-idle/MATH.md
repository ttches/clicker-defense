## 1. The two-sided combat model

NGU Idle has **two separate combat systems**, and the reason it is in this research
set is the **two-sided boss-fight model**: your HP scales with your *own attack*,
the boss's HP scales with the *boss's attack*, and the two sides subtract the
other's defense.

**Boss fights** (the ladder that gates content; `ngu-wiki-boss`, `ngu-wiki-attack`,
`ngu-wiki-defense`):

- `player HP = 10 × player Attack` and `player regen = player Defense / 20`.
- Damage you deal per second `= max(0, player Attack − boss Defense)`.
- Damage you take per second `= max(0, boss Attack − player Defense)` — **zero**
  once your Defense exceeds the boss's Attack.
- **Safe-nuke rule:** the instant-kill ("nuke") button requires
  `player Defense ≥ 5 × boss Attack`.
- Every boss's own HP equals `10 ×` its own Attack (`hp_is_10x_attack` in
  `bosses.csv`: all 190 rows are `yes`).

**Adventure mode** (mob farming; `ngu-wiki-itopod`, `ngu-wiki-skills`,
`ngu-wiki-respawn`) is power/toughness based:

- Idle damage `= random(0.8, 1.2) × (Power − enemy.Defense / 2) × idle_bonus × power_mult`.
- `idle_bonus` = **1.2** base → **1.5** with the Spoopy set → **1.8** with the
  SADISTIC No-Equipment-Challenge.
- Attack cadence: **Idle Mode every 1.0 s** (0.8 s with the Red Liquid set);
  manual skills share a **0.8 s global move cooldown**; enemies respawn at
  **4.0 s** base, floored at ~**0.34 s** (tick-rounded), for a minimum idle-kill
  interval of ~**1.14 s**.

## 2. Boss ladder and scaling

`bosses.csv` holds **190** boss fights for which the wiki's per-enemy
`{{Enemy}}` infobox carries structured `bf_*` stats (Attack / Defense / HP /
HP-regen). The `Boss Fights` page itself lists **301** bosses, but only bosses
1–190 have structured per-page stats; the rest are prose only (see §9 D2).

**Scaling rule** (`ngu-wiki-boss`, verbatim): *"From boss 5 to boss 20, the
current boss will have 5x the stats of the previous boss. This multiplier is
increased to 10x for boss 21 onwards."* The extractor re-derives this from the
page's own anchors (boss 4 = `13e5 / 7e5 / 13e6`) and records `ladder_match` per
row. The rule is the page's own MediaWiki arithmetic — bosses 5–22 are written on
the page as `{{#expr:13e5*5^k}}`, e.g. boss 20 `13e5*5^16`, boss 21
`13e6*5^16` = boss 20 × 10.

Worked anchors: boss 1 `5e4 / 4e4 / 5e5`, boss 4 `1.3e6 / 7e5 / 1.3e7`,
boss 20 `1.984e17 / 1.068e17 / 1.984e18`, boss 21 (×10) `1.984e18 / 1.068e18 /
1.984e19`. The single ladder mismatch is **boss 4** on the per-enemy page
(§9 D1).

## 6. Prestige — the NUMBER

Rebirthing resets the run in exchange for a **NUMBER** multiplier, whose boss
factor is `2^(bosses defeated this rebirth)` in Normal, `1.5^…` in Evil and
`1.2^…` in SADISTIC (`ngu-wiki-number`). Evil divides boss Attack/Defense by
`1e30`; SADISTIC uses the same `1e30` divisor ("the same as Evil as of patch
1.110", `ngu-wiki-sadistic`). The full NUMBER is a product of current/prior boss
factors, a rebirth-time factor, a training-level factor and several subsystem
bonuses.

## 8. Items (artifact)

`items.csv` is the only artifact-lane table: **1,983 stat lines across 390 items
with stats** (394 datamined entries; 4 set items carry no stats), parsed from the
gear-optimizer datamine. Columns: `id, name, slot, set,
zone_index, titan_version, stat, base_value, item_level, scaled_value, source`.
`base_value` is the raw datamined stat; `scaled_value = base × (1 + level/100)`
with `item_level = 100` (the constructor default in `ItemAux.js`, i.e. ×2). The
full 1,983-row table is **not** rendered here (size); read the CSV. This table is
used to spot-check combat-item Power/Toughness scaling, not as a formula source.

## 9. Disputes

**D1 — boss 4 stats conflict (enemy page vs Boss_Fights).** The per-enemy infobox
for *A Small Mouse* gives `bf_power/bf_toughness/bf_hp = 1.1e6 / 6e5 / 1.1e7`,
but the `Boss Fights` page writes boss 4 as `{{BigNum|13e5|3}} /
{{BigNum|7e5|3}} / {{BigNum|13e6|3}}` = **1.3e6 / 7e5 / 1.3e7**. The page wins,
because boss 5 is `6.5e6 = 1.3e6 × 5`, so the ladder base must be 1.3e6; a base of
1.1e6 would make boss 5 `5.5e6`. `bosses.csv` records both (`page_*` columns) and
flags `ladder_match=no` for boss 4. **Flagged for verification against a save.**

**D2 — 301 bosses listed, 190 structured.** `ngu-wiki-boss` lists bosses 1–301,
but only bosses 1–190 have per-page `bf_*` stats. Bosses 191+ are wiki-prose or
rounded scientific notation; `bosses.csv` stops at 190 (a coverage gap, not a
conflict). The wiki also shows prose for bosses 23–30 that matches the ×10 rule.

**D3 — "HP regain is defense/20" is the player's regen.** The `Boss Fights`
sentence describes the **player's** HP/regen (`10 × player Attack`, `player
Defense / 20`). The `bf_hp_regen` field in `bosses.csv` is the **boss's** regen
and follows a different constant. Do not conflate the two.

**D4 — Titan unlock numbers differ by 1 between two wiki pages.** The `Titans`
table gives TIPPI unlock **boss 295** and THE TRAITOR **boss 300**; the
`Boss Fights` unlock table gives **296** and **301**. `titans.csv` records the
`Titans`-table values (`unlock_boss`); one of the two pages is off by one
(likely "reach boss N" vs "beat boss N"). **Flagged.**

**D5 — `bosses.csv` transcribed values are 3–4 significant figures.** The
per-enemy `bf_*` numbers are rounded (e.g. `1.984E+17`); the extractor therefore
compares them to the exact ladder formula at a 1% relative tolerance. The boss-4
mismatch (~15%) is far outside rounding and is real.

**D6 — Titan PPP cross-page conflict (IT HUNGERS / ROCK LOBSTER).** The
`ITOPOD` §*Other income* table gives IT HUNGERS **600,000** and ROCK LOBSTER
**800,000** PPP (`ngu-wiki-itopod`, `ITOPOD.wiki` L415–419); the per-titan
`{{Infobox Titan}}` `pp=` field gives **500,000** and **700,000**
(`ngu-wiki-titan-template`). `titans.csv#pp` transcribes the **per-titan
infobox** (the more specific source, scoped to the titan itself), so the table
and CSV show 500,000/700,000 while the ITOPOD page shows 600,000/800,000.
Both pages **agree** on The Beast 250,000 / The Godmother 300,000 / The Exile
400,000 / AMALGAMATE 1,000,000, so the mismatch is confined to these two
titans. **Recorded, not smoothed; the correct value is `unverifiable` without
the binary.** If the infobox is right, the ITOPOD table is stale; if ITOPOD is
right, the infoboxes are stale.
