## 0. Shape of the game

- **Genre:** pure incremental. **No win/lose condition**; the run is endless and
  progress is measured in score magnitude and reset-layer counts
  (source: ri-wiki/Guide:Pre_Infinity — no failure state is described; only
  "continue upgrading … until your score reaches 1.79e308").
- **Layers (in order of reach):** Revolution (Circles) → **Prestige** →
  **Promote** → **Infinity** → **Eternity** → **Unity**. `dump.cs` names the
  three *major* reset types `PrestigeType { Infinity, Eternity, Unity }`
  (`dump.cs:293778`); the wiki calls Infinity/Eternity/Unity the "major reset
  layers" and counts them 3rd/4th/5th overall after prestige/promote
  (source: ri-wiki/Infinity, ri-wiki/Eternity, ri-wiki/Unity).
- **Persistent state model:** one `GameData` object holds the whole save and
  nests per-layer data — `infinity`, `eternity`, `unity`, `attacks`, `minerals`,
  `elements`, `tarot`, `plague`, `singularity`, `automation`, `macro` — plus the
  score/currency/time fields (`dump.cs:293427-293536`). `GameController`
  exposes them as static getters (`dump.cs:289300-289309`). Save numbers are
  wrapped in `ObscuredDouble`/`ObscuredInt` (anti-memory-edit), and a
  `BigDouble` type carries values past `double` range
  (`dump.cs:308878`, `dump.cs:680106`).

## 2. Assets / generators — the 10 Circles (checklist §2, §4)

The base producers are **Circles** (a.k.a. Colors): 10 of them, unlocked in
fixed order once the previous Circle has **5 levels** bought, starting with Red
(which starts at 5 levels) (source: ri-wiki/Revolution). A Circle is a
`Revolution` object with `progress`, `ascension`, `mult`, `slowdown`,
`speed`, `multGain` fields (`dump.cs:296885-296906`).

| # | Circle | Initial cost (ⵙ) | Cost multiplier / level | Base lap speed (laps/s per level) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Red | 4 | 1.20 | 0.200 |
| 2 | Orange | 100 | 1.24 | 0.100 |
| 3 | Yellow | 1,000 | 1.28 | 0.067 |
| 4 | Green | 10,000 | 1.32 | 0.050 |
| 5 | Turquoise | 1,000,000 | 1.36 | 0.040 |
| 6 | Cyan | 1e9 | 1.40 | 0.033 |
| 7 | Blue | 1e12 | 1.44 | 0.029 |
| 8 | Purple | 1e15 | 1.48 | 0.025 |
| 9 | Pink | 1e18 | 1.52 | 0.022 |
| 10 | White | 1e27 | 1.56 | 0.020 |

(source: ri-wiki/Revolution; cost table transcribed verbatim.)

- **Cost curve:** each level multiplies cost by that Circle's multiplier
  (`Buyable.costInc`, `dump.cs:292488`); so it is a **pure exponential
  (geometric) curve** per Circle, with the multiplier *rising* +0.1 per
  ascension (see §3). Smaller Circles are strictly cheaper-and-faster early;
  the wiki's buy rule is "prioritize upgrading smaller circle that you can
  afford, and wait until the price difference between the bigger and smaller
  circles has disappeared" (source: ri-wiki/Revolution).
- **Holding cap:** not unbounded — each Circle's buyable level is capped at
  **100 + 10 × ascensions**; the cap rises by 10 per Ascension
  (source: ri-wiki/Revolution). This is a hard level wall, not a level count of
  Circles.
- **"Lap speed" is a rate, not a timer:** increasing level raises the Circle's
  laps/s; each completed lap both pays Score and adds to that Circle's `mult`
  (by `multGain`). The displayed color number *is* the accumulated multiplier
  (source: ri-wiki/Revolution).

## 3. Income formula (checklist §3, §4c)

Base loop (per ri-wiki/Revolution, "Income Calculation"):

```
ⵙ/second  =  ⵙ/Revolution × [ Σ over all Circles of (Revolutions/second) ]
ⵙ/Revolution = ( Π over all Circles of Mult × P.Mult ) ^ Common Exponent
```

- **With Prestige** the formula is stated as
  `ⵙ/Revolution = ( Π Mult × P.Mult ) ^ P.Exponent`
  (source: ri-wiki/Revolution) — i.e. prestige swaps the generic `Common
  Exponent` for the player's `P.Exponent` in the "before prestige" formula.
  (These two wiki statements conflict about which exponent applies when; see
  **Disputes**.)
- **Common Exponent** ("applied last, extremely powerful") is the master scaling
  dial; the wiki explicitly says "Common exponent is influenced by many factors
  in the game so a formula wouldn't be easy to follow" — treat its exact value
  as **not fully specified by the wiki** (source: ri-wiki/Revolution).
- **Full soul-shop form (wiki):**
  `Income = (Income-per-Revolution × (1 + Soul-Income Boost)) × Σ Rev/s`
  where
  `Base Revolution Mult = (Π Color Mults) × Prestige Mult × (1+Soul P.Mult) × (1+Soul Multiplier) × (1+Soul Income Mult)`
  and `Income per Revolution = Base Revolution Mult ^ Common Exponent`
  (source: ri-wiki/Revolution). Note the additive `(1+shop)` terms — soul-shop
  income boosts are **additive within the base-mult product**, then the whole
  thing is raised to the exponent.
- **This is a derivative/compound scaling family (§4c):** Circles feed Mult,
  Mult is exponentiated, and Generators (Infinity layer) feed *Generator Power*
  which multiplies Mult Gain (`Generator.genMult`, `dump.cs:293896`). Each reset
  layer adds another multiplicative term *inside or outside* the exponent.

## 4. Ascension (per-Circle mini-reset)

- Triggered when a Circle reaches its level cap. Effects (source:
  ri-wiki/Revolution):
  - resets that Circle to **level 5** (not 0),
  - multiplies its **Mult Gain by Ascension Power** (default **×10**, "improved
    later with future upgrades"),
  - raises its level cap by **+10**, and
  - raises its cost multiplier by **+0.1**.
  - The **initial price after ascension equals the cost at level 100 before
    ascension** (cost is continuous across the reset).
- Worked example on the wiki: Orange after 1 ascension at level 10 =
  `100 × 1.24^100 × 1.34^10 ≈ 4.1e12 ⵙ` (source: ri-wiki/Revolution).
- `GameController.ascPower` (`dump.cs:289260`) and `Revolution.Ascend()`
  (`dump.cs:296930`) corroborate Ascension Power is a global BigDouble.

## 5. Prestige

- **Unlock:** Score reaches **1e10 (10B)** (source: ri-wiki/Revolution).
- **Grants:** `P.Mult` (static multiplier, sits after White's mult) and
  `P.Exponent` (affects the common exponent / logarithmic scaling of income).
- **Reset scope:** **everything** — accumulated Score, Circle upgrades and
  Ascensions (source: ri-wiki/Revolution). Saved as arrays
  `GameData.prestigeExp` / `prestigeMult` and computed `pMult`
  (`dump.cs:293514-293517`).
- **Re-gate:** after prestiging you must climb back to the score at which you
  last prestiged before the Prestige window is available again.
- **Formulae (verbatim wikitext `action=raw`, captured in `tools/revolution-idle/ri-wiki.mjs`; see Disputes D1 for the log base):**
  - `P.mult = 2.56 ^ ( log10(score / 10^3) / 2.25 )`
  - `P.exp  = 1 + log10(score / 10^5) / 225`
- **Designed cadence:** prestige at ~×20 P.Mult the first time, then each time
  at least ~100× / one-or-two orders of magnitude higher than the last
  (source: ri-wiki/Revolution; ri-wiki/Guide:Pre_Infinity).

## 6. Promotions (Promote) — 4 boost types

- **Unlock:** first available when `P.Mult` exceeds **×1000** (on-hand or
  upcoming) (source: ri-wiki/Revolution).
- **Action:** resets *all progress to this point* and levels up **one** of four
  boosts (you choose; you cannot pick a boost you can't afford a level of):
  1. **Mult Gain Mult** — multiplies Circles' Mult Gain.
  2. **Laps Speed Mult** — multiplies Circles' laps/s.
  3. **Ascension Power** — enhances Circles' Asc. Power.
  4. **Promotion Power** — boosts the effectiveness of boosts 1–3.
  (source: ri-wiki/Revolution)
- **XP gained** from the P.Mult value: `Promotion.xp = floor((P.mult/1000)^0.75)`
  (source: ri-wiki/Revolution). `Promotion` class carries `level`, `effect`,
  `xpNow`/`xpNext`, and a 4-element `GameData.promotions` array
  (`dump.cs:292433-292445`, `dump.cs:293530`).
- **Milestones table** (clear on the wiki; full 0–20 row set belongs in MATH.md):
  at level 0 the four boosts read 1 / 1.000 / 10.00 / 1.000; the wiki lists
  effect, cumulative XP, and the P.Mult needed to buy the next level (e.g.
  lvl 1 costs 1,000 P.Mult, lvl 2 costs 4,326, … lvl 20 costs 1.07e11)
  (source: ri-wiki/Revolution).
- **Recommended order:** 1→2→3→4 repeating; ~level 75 on all four before
  Infinity (source: ri-wiki/Revolution). `GameController.aprom_prom` /
  `aprom_lv` / `aprom_time` fields are the Auto-Promote settings
  (`dump.cs:289262-289264`).

## 7. Infinity (major reset #1)

- **Trigger:** reach **1.79e308 Score** (≈2^1024) → claim IP → Infinity
  (source: ri-wiki/Infinity).
- **Reset scope:** Score; Colors + their Ascensions; Prestige Mult/Exponent;
  Promotion levels; **Generator Power** (but bought Generator *amounts* are
  kept); **Stardust** (source: ri-wiki/Infinity).
- **Resources:** `IP` (spendable) and `∞` (one per Infinity, never spent).
  Saved as `InfinityData.IP`, `.infs`, `.generators`, `.challenges`,
  `.genPower`, `.genExp`, `.genMult`, `.ownedInfUpgrades`, `.starUpgrades`,
  `.stardust`, `.stars`, `.starExp` (`dump.cs:294147-294184`).
- **Infinity Upgrades (IU):** a **skill tree** (rows/columns e.g. `1;1`,
  `18;1`), 42 upgrades; costs from 1 IP up to 1e33 IP; effects include
  automation unlocks, +Ascension Power, gen boosts, and
  `Efficiency V` (Gen Exponent base → 0.75), `Almost one` (→ 0.9)
  (source: ri-wiki/Infinity). `InfinityData.InfUpgradeUnlocked(TAxis)`
  confirms a 2-D axis grid (`dump.cs:294240`).
- **Generators:** G1 produces **Generator Power (GP)**; GP multiplies Mult Gain.
  GP is raised to a base exponent **0.666** by default (improved by IU 14;2,
  19;1, 20;2). G2 produces G1, G3 produces G2, G4→G3, "and so on". **Buying a
  Generator multiplies its interval by 2×**, and **Generator Mult soft-caps at
  1e1,000** (source: ri-wiki/Infinity). Fields: `Generator.genMult`, `income`,
  `allCost`, `costInc` (`dump.cs:293884-293899`).
- **Infinity Challenges (IC):** 9 challenges, each with a handicap and a
  challenge-specific reward plus **+1 to the IP-gain multiplier**; unlocked by
  IU 7;1. After all 9 → **Break Infinity**, allowing IP past the 1.79e308 cap
  (source: ri-wiki/Infinity). Best challenge *times* matter: later IU
  (15;2-4, 16;3, 17;2) scale with **lower** times (source: ri-wiki/Infinity).
  Note: challenge times are scaled by Time Flux game-speed, so "instantly
  completed" times must be set at 1× (source: ri-wiki/Infinity,
  ri-wiki/Time_Flux).
- **Break Infinity threshold:** at **e3,080 Score** the 10× IP bar fills; every
  further **+e308** of Score gives **×10** IP gain for that run
  (source: ri-wiki/Infinity).
- **Stars (last Infinity tab):** unlocked by IU **21;1** (cost 1e33 IP); Stars
  generate **Stardust** which enhances Generators and GP gain. Star cost
  starts at 1e33 and grows by ×1e3 then ×1e7+; upgrades are **Star Base**
  (`2.75 + 0.275·n`) and **Star Exponent** (`0.4 + 0.05·n`). Four Stardust
  Upgrades exist; Stardust resets to 0 each Infinity. Stars/Star upgrades are
  prioritized **over** Generators (source: ri-wiki/Infinity). Fields:
  `InfinityData.stars`, `.baseStarBase`, `.starExp`, `.starCost`,
  `.stardustUpgrades` (`dump.cs:294170-294184`).

## 8. Eternity (major reset #2)

- **Trigger:** **~1.79e308 IP** (2^1024 IP) → Eternate for EP
  (source: ri-wiki/Eternity).
- **Reset scope:** Infinity Upgrades; bought Generators; IC completions +
  fastest times + time sum; fastest Infinity; Stars and Star Upgrades; and
  everything Infinity already resets (source: ri-wiki/Eternity).
- **Resources:** `EP` (spendable) and `Σ` Eternities (never spent, milestone
  key). Initially **1 EP / 1 Σ per Eternate**; more EP per Eternate requires
  **Eternity Milestone 10 ("Break Eternity")**, and >1 Σ per Eternate requires
  having United once (source: ri-wiki/Eternity).
- **EP cap → Unity:** EP is initially limited to **1.08e2466 EP**, the exact
  amount required for Unity (source: ri-wiki/Eternity).
- **Eternity Milestones** (by Σ count): 1→unlock; 2→keep Automations; 3→Animal
  Zoo; 5→buying Circles costs no Score; 7→Auto IP Adjuster; 8→Eternity
  Challenges; 12→auto Infinity tree; 14→auto Generators; 16→auto Stars +
  Auto Eternity; 32→**Break Eternity**; 100→×1.05 IP gain per Eternity
  (soft-capped at 1,000 Σ) (source: ri-wiki/Eternity). Flags live in
  `EternityData.eternityMilestones` (`dump.cs:293052`).
- **Eternity Bonuses (6):** Mult gain, Lap speed, Ascension Power, Infinity
  gain, Generator power, Stardust gain — all improve with Σ; starting values
  ×2 / ×2 / ×1.1 / ×2 / ×2.14 / ×10 (source: ri-wiki/Eternity). Exact
  piecewise formulae are on the wiki ("Statistics for nerds") and are strongly
  non-linear above Σ=10–20; MATH.md should carry them.
- **Animals:** 81 animals, total **1,524 AP** to buy all;
  respec-friendly (respec resets the *current* Eternity)
  (source: ri-wiki/Eternity). Each animal grants a permanent bonus (IP gain,
  Score exponent, Generator exponent, Star Base, Ascension Power, Stardust
  exponent, …). `EternityData.ownedAnimals`, `.AP`, `.APAmmoEP/IP/SC`
  (`dump.cs:293059-293076`).
  - **Animal Milestones** (by # owned): 1→Slowdown; 4→Auto Slowdown; 7→Lab;
    10→Promoting resets nothing; 20→passive IP = 100% of Infinity payout/s;
    48→+10 free RP levels; 81→unlock Dilation (source: ri-wiki/Eternity).
  - **Slowdown:** spend lap speed (÷10) for **+1 Ascension** to all Circles;
    resets on Infinity; reversible (Speedup) (source: ri-wiki/Eternity).
- **Laboratory:** unlocked at Animal Milestone 3. Spend EP on **Base
  (+0.20/lvl), Mult (+0.50/lvl), Power (+0.01/lvl)**; LP/s = `(Base × Mult)^Power`;
  filling the flask grants **1 RP**. Six RP upgrades: IP Gain ×4/lvl; Ascend
  Power `×(1+0.25n)`; Star Base Mult `×(1+0.15n)`; EP Gain `×(1+0.5n)`; Gen
  Power Exponent +0.02/lvl; Common Exponent +0.01/lvl (**soft-capped at level
  500**). RP respeccable at the cost of a new Eternity (source:
  ri-wiki/Eternity). Fields: `labPtsNow`, `curRP`, `laboratoryUpgrades`,
  `RsPUpgrades` (`dump.cs:293106-293123`).
- **Eternity Challenges (EC):** 10 challenges × 5 difficulties = 50, plus a 6th
  difficulty per challenge after **Insane Trial 4** = **60** total; goal is a
  target Score under a penalty; each clear gives **+1 free AP** and a
  "magnet chance" multiplier beyond 50 (source: ri-wiki/Eternity).
  - **EC Milestones:** 3→keep ICs; 5→Prestige resets nothing; 8→Supernova;
    10→keep fastest Infinity; 30/32/35→free RP levels per N Supernovae;
    50→Dilation Tree; 60→2× magnet chance (source: ri-wiki/Eternity).
- **Supernova:** unlocked at EC-milestone 3 (8 EC completions). "Go Supernova"
  starts a new Eternity and grants bonuses: Eternity-reward Mult, Star Base
  Mult, Lab Base Mult, EP gain Mult, + AP. Its Score cost has a piecewise/
  tetration formula and **hard-caps at 10^(10^256) Score** (source:
  ri-wiki/Eternity). Fields: `supernovaLv`, `supernovaReq`, `supernovaBonuses`
  (`dump.cs:293128-293131`).
- **Dilation:** unlocked at Animal Milestone 7 (all 81 animals). Entering
  starts a new Eternity with penalties (Common Exp ÷25, Gen Exp ÷1000, Star
  Exp ÷100, Asc Power ^0.10, Laps speed ^0.10 then ÷10, IP gain ^0.01).
  Exiting sets Max Score to the max reached in Dilation, which drives **DP
  income**. **9 Dilation Upgrades** (weaken penalties, boost DP, free RP
  levels, Common Exp mult, Supernova power, Gen Exp mult, EP mult, Asc Power
  mult, Lab power mult); DU1–3 effect-per-level can be boosted by DTP nodes
  (source: ri-wiki/Eternity). Fields: `inDilation`, `dilationMaxScore`, `DP`,
  `dilationUpgrades`, and the six `dilation*Penalty` doubles
  (`dump.cs:293134-293155`).
- **Dilation Tree:** unlocked by all 50 ECs. Buy **DTP** with DP; allocate to
  **13 Dilation Tree Upgrades** across Center + 3 axes (Top/Middle/Bottom) × 4;
  each DTU buyable up to **5×**, **65 DTP** to max all. Tree must be bought in
  order (1 DTP in a parent unlocks the next tier). Respec zeroes DP and max
  Dilation score and starts a new Eternity (source: ri-wiki/Eternity). Fields:
  `dilationTree`, `dtpBought`, `dtpSpent` (`dump.cs:293157-293161`).

## 9. Unity (major reset #3)

- **Trigger:** **1.08e2466 EP** (source: ri-wiki/Unity). On first Unity you
  choose **one Zodiac** from Fire/Wind/Water/Earth; the first Unity is
  guaranteed a **Lv3 Rare Aries** with Common-Exponent + Mult-Gain buffs
  (source: ri-wiki/Unity).
- **Unity Bonus multipliers (passive, scale with Ʊ):** IP Gain, EP Gain, Σ Gain,
  DP Gain (explicit formulas on the wiki, e.g. `IP gain = [1+Unities]^[2+log(Unities)]`)
  (source: ri-wiki/Unity). Saved as `UnityData.unities`, `.unityBonuses`
  (`dump.cs:302718`, `dump.cs:302747`).
- **Astrology:** Zodiacs (12 signs, 4 elements, each a season) with Quality /
  Level / Rarity; assigned to **Planets** (one Zodiac per Planet) to activate
  bonuses. **Planet Shop** (after Easy Trial 6) spends Astrodust on donuts
  (Game speed, Luck, Quality, Zodiac Exp Factor) and modifiers (Merging,
  Enhancing, Redistribution, Sacrificing) (source: ri-wiki/Unity). Fields:
  `UnityData.inventory`, `.planetsInventory`, `.planets`, `.luck`,
  `.baseQuality`, `.astrodust`, `.donuts`, `.spaceship` (`dump.cs:302727-302767`).
- **Trials:** Unity's equivalent of Eternity Challenges — **25 trials** (6 Easy,
  6 Medium, 6 Hard, 6 Insane, 1 Bonus); each gives a permanent reward and **+2
  default EC completions**; completing a difficulty tier gives another reward.
  Trials are "locked" (source: ri-wiki/Unity). Fields: `UnityData.trial`,
  `.completeTrials`, `.ecSaved` (`dump.cs:302776-302779`).
- **Relics:** **70** unique relics bought with **Gold** from Attack runs,
  unlocking at Attack levels (source: ri-wiki/Unity; `Relic` class
  `dump.cs:297722`).
- **Attacks:** unlocked after **15 trials**. Adds **5 Attack Circles** (like the
  base circles); each lap increases the Attack Damage Multiplier **and** counts
  as one hit on the current enemy's HP bar; clearing advances the Attack Level.
  A proper Unity resets Attack Level to 0 and pays Gold; circle attack
  multipliers persist through Unity (source: ri-wiki/Unity). Fields:
  `AttacksData`, `AttacksLevel`, `AttacksRevolution` (`dump.cs:297549-297833`).
- **Minerals:** unlocked by Achievement 240 (Attack level 1337). **100
  minerals**; each yields **Value Points**; base VP gain `0.01·3^(MineralLevel−1)`.
  At mineral level 30 → **Polish Prestige** (PP → 5 weapons: Sword/Axe/Spear/
  Bow/Knuckles). At level 70 → **Refinement Prestige** (RfP → 136-node Refine
  Tree). Refine nodes gate Magnets, Runes (Sun/Moon), Special Minerals, SMP,
  Sacrifice (source: ri-wiki/Unity). Fields: `MineralsData`,
  `MineralsUpgrade` (`dump.cs:299155`, `dump.cs:298472`).
- **Elements:** unlocked by Achievement 321. Generate **one** of Fire/Earth/
  Wind/Water at a time; each boosted by element-specific Factors and a Global
  Element Multiplier; spent on Element Trees (source: ri-wiki/Unity; `Element`,
  `ElementNode`, `ElementFactor` `dump.cs:298022-298195`).
- **Tarot:** unlocked via Refine Tree Node 70. Black Gems → **Tarot Draws**;
  **78 cards** (4 regular suits of 14 + 22 Arcana); regular suits generate
  resources (unlock Tarot Upgrades at 1e6 each), each card has a Challenge,
  and a flush converts resources to golden for **Artifacts** (source:
  ri-wiki/Unity; `TarotData`/`TarotCards`/`TarotArtifact`
  `dump.cs:300974-301961`).
- **Plague:** unlocked via Refine Tree Node 103. Infect populations per Plague
  Stage → **Plague Generators (PlG)** → **Plague Points (PlP)**; craft
  Endoplasmic Reticulums (Spread Power / Infectivity / Stealth); dissolving
  gives Virus Essence → **ViP** (source: ri-wiki/Unity; `PlagueData`,
  `PlagueSimulation.*` `dump.cs:299600-300101`).
- **Singularity:** unlocked via Refine Tree Node 121. **Atoms** self-multiply
  per second by the Singularity Multiplier; a **Singularity** resets Minerals
  (except Refine Tree), Elements, Tarot (except Upgrades/Challenges), Plague,
  and Atom count. After the first, each Singularity needs **1.8e308 Atoms**.
  Milestones (1st), Effects (2nd), Tree of 13 nodes × up to level 10 with
  Ascension (5), Houses for Singularity Zodiacs (32) (source: ri-wiki/Unity;
  `SingularityData`, `SingularityTreeNode`, `SingularHouse`
  `dump.cs:300251-300811`).

## 10. Soul Shop (meta-progression / IAP)

- **Unlock:** after **5 minutes of unscaled playtime** (source: ri-wiki/Soul_Shop).
- **Sells:** multiplicative **Boosts** per layer (Revolution, Prestige,
  Infinity, Eternity, Macro Builder, Unity, Minerals, Time Flux), TF hours,
  QoL (Zodiac Slots, Macro Slots), and cosmetic **Skins**
  (source: ri-wiki/Soul_Shop).
- Boosts are stated to be **multiplicative with each other** unless noted.
  Notable caps: Zodiac Slots cap **100**; Macro Slots cap **100**; Macro
  Blocks cap **10,000** (source: ri-wiki/Soul_Shop).
- **Total soul cost** (wiki): all boosts + skins = **1,930,000**; including
  Zodiac/Macro slots+blocks = **12,425,000** Souls (source: ri-wiki/Soul_Shop).
- Souls are earnable F2P (codes ≈ once each, daily rewards ≈232/day,
  achievements up to 37,675 total as of v1.034) (source: ri-wiki/Soul_Shop).
  `RewardType { Souls, TimeFlux, Skin }` (`dump.cs:293943`).

## 11. Automations & Macro Builder (checklist §10 "reduced attention")

- **Automations** are QoL unlocks gated by achievements / trials / milestones /
  the Infinity tree, spanning every layer: Autobuy Buyables (IU 1;1), Auto
  Ascend (2;2), Auto Prestige (5;3), Auto Promote (10;1), Auto Infinity (15;1);
  IP Adjuster (Σ7), auto Infinity-tree/generators/stars, Auto Eternity (Σ16),
  Auto Slowdown (Animal ms 2); Autobuy AP/Animals/Lab/RP, Auto Supernova, Auto
  DTP, auto Dilation upgrades, Auto-sell Zodiacs, Auto Unity, mineral/attack/
  relic automations (source: ri-wiki/Automations).
- **Macro Builder:** unlocked on first Eternity. A **block-based scripting**
  system (30 block types: conditionals If/Else/Else If, loops Repeat/Repeat
  Until, awaiters Wait For/Until, game-event blocks Prestige/Promote/Infinite/
  challenge-enter/Eternate/Dilate/Tarot/etc., notifications). Starts with
  **3 macros × 30 blocks**; extra slots buyable with Souls (5,000 each);
  loop modes Once/Loop/Ping Pong; Startup Execution; **Export/Import as
  "revoscript"** (source: ri-wiki/Macro_Builder).
- This is the clearest "one-shot macro automation replaces repetitive resets"
  design: the game expects players to script IC/EC farming, Unity farming,
  attack grinding, etc. (source: ri-wiki/Automations "Some useful Macros").

## 12. Offline / catch-up (checklist §3, §8)

- **Time Flux (TF) and Offline Flux (OF)** both accrue **only while the game is
  not running** (on mobile, even in background) (source: ri-wiki/Time_Flux).
- **Game Speed:** spend TF to run the simulation at **2×–60×**; `n×` costs
  `(n−1)` seconds of TF per real second; when TF empties it reverts to 1×.
  **Automations degrade at high speed** because the tick rate is fixed, so
  challenge-time records should be set at 1× (source: ri-wiki/Time_Flux).
- **OF** is stored and consumed at startup; TF↔OF conversion rate **3:5**
  (1h TF → 1h40m OF); OF capacity is much larger (24h initial vs 1h TF).
  Players can split or auto-use it; the wiki recommends **not** auto-using
  because the game needs ~20s to settle (source: ri-wiki/Time_Flux).
- **Acquisition:** base OF gain **10 min/hour**; base TF gain can convert at
  5/3 (cap makes total OF 60 min/h, so ≤30 min TF/h converts). TF Capacity and
  TF Gain are upgraded with TF (TF Gain lvl formula `3600·(n+1)/(n+10)`
  sec/hour; cost `1800·1.3^n`); **Online TF gain** only starts at TF Gain
  level 8 (source: ri-wiki/Time_Flux).
- A 30-minute TF bonus is given on first game entry (source: ri-wiki/Time_Flux).
- **Offline catch-up is a *speed* resource, not a per-second accrual** — a
  notable variant vs. Cookie Clicker's offline CpS (§8 of the taxonomy).

## 13. Caps, walls & failure modes (checklist §12)

- **No lose condition / no "end"** — progression is endless; the wiki frames
  goals as reaching each layer's trigger (source: ri-wiki/Guide:Pre_Infinity,
  ri-wiki/Guide:Getting_Started).
- **Numeric walls are the layer triggers** (each doubles as a soft cap):
  Score **1.79e308** → Infinity; IP **1.79e308** → Eternity; EP **1.08e2466** →
  Unity (source: ri-wiki/Infinity, ri-wiki/Eternity, ri-wiki/Unity).
- **Break thresholds** remove the layer caps: Break Infinity (all 9 ICs; IP
  uncapped; e308 Score increments ×10 IP), Break Eternity (Σ32; EP scales with
  IP). (source: ri-wiki/Infinity, ri-wiki/Eternity)
- **Named soft caps:** Generator Mult **1e1,000** (source: ri-wiki/Infinity);
  Lab Common-Exponent RP upgrade **level 500** (source: ri-wiki/Eternity);
  Eternity-milestone-11 IP bonus soft-capped at **1,000 Σ**; Supernova Score
  cost **hard-caps 10^(10^256)** (source: ri-wiki/Eternity); DU effects 4/5/9
  soft-cap (source: ri-wiki/Eternity); RP6 softcap flag
  `EternityData.rp6softcap` (`dump.cs:293124`).
- **Anti-autoclicker / anti-cheat:** values are `ObscuredDouble`/`ObscuredInt`
  and saves can be marked (ban reason field `GameData.banReason`,
  `dump.cs:293450`); a `GameBanana`/`click` field exists (`dump.cs:293830`).
  No click-supply cap mechanic was found — this game has **no manual click
  lane**, only lap-driven production.
- **Discounts:** no explicit discount/price-reduction currency was found in the
  wiki pages reviewed; ascent raises (not lowers) circle cost multipliers, so
  the taxonomy §5 "100% discount" hazard does not apply here.

## 14. Unlock schedule / first-hour shape (checklist §13)

Base loop gates (source: ri-wiki/Revolution, ri-wiki/Infinity,
ri-wiki/Eternity, ri-wiki/Unity, ri-wiki/Guide:Pre_Infinity):

1. Start: Red Circle, 5 levels.
2. Each next Circle unlocks after **5 levels** of the previous (Red→…→White).
3. **Ascension** available per Circle at its level cap (100 + 10·asc).
4. **Prestige** at 1e10 Score.
5. **Promotion** when P.Mult > ×1000.
6. **Infinity** at 1.79e308 Score (≈2^1024); IU challenges after IU 7; Break
   Infinity after all 9 ICs; Stars after IU 21;1 (1e33 IP).
7. **Eternity** at 1.79e308 IP; milestones at Σ=1,2,3,5,7,8,12,14,16,32,100;
   Animals at Σ3; Lab at Σ3 + 7 animals; ECs at Σ8; Supernova at 8 EC; Dilation
   at 81 animals; Dilation Tree at 50 EC.
8. **Unity** at 1.08e2466 EP; Trials; Attacks after 15 trials; Relics; Minerals
   (Ach 240); Elements (Ach 321); Tarot/Plague/Singularity via Refine Tree
   nodes.
9. **Soul Shop** at 5 min unscaled playtime; **Macro Builder** on first
   Eternity; **Leaderboard** at 1e6 Score (source: ri-wiki/Revolution).

- **Reward clock:** the base cadence is lap completion (sub-second early), then
  buyable purchases, then per-layer resets. Because layers are gated by
  *magnitude* triggers, the work→reward interval naturally lengthens over a run
  (consistent with the theory track's "reward frequency" dial).

## 15. Endgame & reward-only layers (checklist §14)

- **No win screen.** The game keeps adding layers; the wiki flags Unity as
  "not yet complete … actively under development" (source: ri-wiki/Unity,
  ri-wiki/Guide:Pre_Infinity).
- **Achievements:** **675** total (source: ri-wiki/Guide:Getting_Started);
  every achievement gives **+0.01× Mult gain**, some give Souls/TF, and some
  gate automations/mechanics (e.g. Relic autobuy at Ach 338, Elements at Ach
  321, Mineral sub-tab at Ach 240). `GameData.unlockedAch` /
  `AchievementBonus` (`dump.cs:293519`, `dump.cs:293534`).
- **Cosmetic layer:** skins purchased in the Soul Shop (Revolution-tab skins;
  `RevolutionSkin` enum `dump.cs:279653`) — a reward-only sink.
- **"Reduced attention" upgrades:** the entire Automations list + Macro Builder
  is the reduced-attention layer (§11).

1. **Common Exponent exact formula** — the wiki explicitly declines to give one
   ("influenced by many factors"); which upgrades/animals/EC rewards/Dilation
   contribute, and in what order, is unresolved. (source: ri-wiki/Revolution)
2. **Prestige `P.exp` string** — is it `1 + log10(score/10^5)^2.25` or a

   clean source or measurement. Likewise the four Promotion formula strings.

   not on the reviewed page; `Generator.baseCost`/`costInc` exist in

5. **Down-weighting / cross-layer interaction order** — the exact order in which

   partly tetration-based with two extra scaling regimes; needs careful

## Disputes

1. **Which exponent applies before/with prestige.** `ri-wiki/Revolution`
   "Income Calculation" states the *pre-prestige* formula uses
   `Common Exponent`, while the immediately following "Income Calculation with
   Prestige" replaces it with `P.Exponent`. These two statements conflict
   (Prestige is itself what produces P.Exponent). Recorded as-is; needs a
   clean re-read of the page's MathML or in-game measurement.
2. **First-prestige cadence vs. P.mult formula.** The wiki's prose says prestige
   first at **×20 P.Mult** (source: ri-wiki/Guide:Pre_Infinity), while a naive
   evaluation of the displayed `P.mult = 2.56·log10(score/10^3)^2.25` at the
   1e10 unlock gives a very different value. The formula retrieval (superscripts
   lost) is suspect — flag for the harvester/verifier.
3. **Skill/level cap wording.** `ri-wiki/Revolution` says a Circle can be
   upgraded "to a maximum of 100+(amount of ascensions x10) levels" and also
   "The maximum level of each buyable increases by 10 per ascension" — these
   agree, but the phrase "raise the level cap by 10" in the Ascension section
   could be misread as a flat +10 forever rather than +10 per ascension. Both
   readings recorded.
